## Feel statement (sensory)
The product should feel like locking a chase on a Vandercook: cold steel under the thumb, the smell of rubber-based ink, bone paper that still remembers the watermark, a strip of acid chartreuse tape marking the gripper edge. Generation is not a spinner — it is the moment the quoin bites and the proof comes up wet. UI chrome stays quiet; the stamped page is the loudest object in the room.
